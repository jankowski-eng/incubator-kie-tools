On the canvas

  • Add an element: drag it from the palette
  • Connect: drag the blue handle of a selected node onto another node
  • Rename: double-click a node, or press F2
  • Delete: the × on a selected node, or the Delete key
  • Move around: scroll to zoom, drag the background to pan
  • Undo / redo: ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z
